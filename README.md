# Packetwise Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 2, 2026

Packetwise helps you record, organize, and manage personal expenses, bills, income, and related financial planning information.

## Information stored in the app

Expense, bill, income, loan, and planning information that you enter is stored locally on your device. Packetwise does not require an account or login, and the developer does not receive this information through the app’s local storage.

## Optional Google Drive backup and sync

If you choose to use Google Drive backup or sync, Packetwise requests the permission needed to create and access its own backup file in your Google Drive. The backup is used only to save and restore your Packetwise data. Google’s privacy policy and terms also apply to your use of Google Drive.

## Advertising

Packetwise uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may process device information, advertising identifiers, approximate information, and ad interaction information in accordance with Google’s policies. See [Google’s partner-sites policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Children’s privacy

Packetwise may be used by people of different ages, including children under 13 with a parent or guardian’s permission. Packetwise does not require children to create an account or submit personal information to the developer. Advertising and other third-party services may apply their own age-based safeguards and policies. Parents or guardians who have questions can contact us using the address below.

## Data security and deletion

You can delete information stored in Packetwise by deleting the relevant records in the app or uninstalling the app. You can also delete Packetwise backup files from your Google Drive. If you need help or want to request deletion of information associated with a support request, contact us.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page with its updated date.
